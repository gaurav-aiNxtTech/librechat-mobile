# Parity_Checklist

Every KMP_App screen and user-visible feature, with its status in the Flutter App (Req 48.8).

- Status is one of `ported`, `todo`, `deferred: <deferral>` (iOS, multi-account, localization, prefetch, home-screen shortcuts or custom accent picker) or `dropped: <reason>` (a mechanism Req 1.1 leaves out).
- Each task flips the rows it delivers to `ported`. The Rewrite is feature-complete when no `todo` rows remain (Req 48.9).
- KMP source paths are shortened: `feature/<m>/…` means `feature/<m>/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/garfiec/librechat/feature/<m>/…`, and likewise for `core/<m>/…` and `shared/…`. `(android)` means the `androidMain` source set. `app/…` means `app/src/main/kotlin/com/garfiec/librechat/…`.
